Simulated dynamic case

A patient that changes as you treat them

Unlike a manual case, a simulated case unfolds in real time. The patient's vitals and condition respond to your decisions — so timing, prioritisation and reassessment all matter.

How it works

1

You meet a live patient

You're handed a presenting complaint and a starting set of vitals — but nothing is fixed. The clock is running from the moment you begin.

2

The condition evolves

As time passes, the patient may deteriorate or stabilise depending on what you do. Order the right investigations and start the right management to change the trajectory.

3

You assess, act, and reassess

Examine, investigate, and treat in any order. Every action has a consequence, and the patient's response tells you whether your reasoning is on track.

4

You get scored on outcomes

At the end you receive feedback on your diagnosis, your management, and how well you kept the patient safe under time pressure.

Once you start, the simulation runs in real time. Try to work as you would on a real shift — there's no pausing the patient.
